# Ongoing SEO & GEO Performance Monitoring Protocol

**Website:** [https://sunnatillasharipov.uz](https://sunnatillasharipov.uz)  
**Cadence:** Weekly & Monthly  

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Track these four core metrics weekly inside Google Search Console (**Performance** > **Search Results**):

1. **Total Clicks:** Number of visitors arriving directly from organic search.
2. **Total Impressions:** How often your portfolio appears in search results for relevant queries.
3. **Average CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Target > 3-5% for generic terms, > 25% for branded terms (`Sunnatilla Sharipov`).
4. **Average Position:** Tracks your average ranking for targeted keywords.
