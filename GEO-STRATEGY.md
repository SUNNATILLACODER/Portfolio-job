## 1. What is GEO and Why Does It Matter?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) ensures that modern AI search engines and Large Language Models (LLMs) can reliably extract, cite, synthesize, and recommend Sunnatilla Sharipov as a verified, high-quality developer.

Unlike traditional SEO which relies solely on links and keyword density, AI engines evaluate:
1. **Entity Clarity & Disambiguation:** Who is the entity? What are their verified names and handles?
2. **Fact Consistency:** Are details consistent across the website, GitHub, and LinkedIn?
3. **Information Gain & Grounding:** Does the page provide factual, direct answers to common queries?
4. **Machine-Readable Structure:** Can LLMs parse Schema.org graphs and semantic HTML without executing heavy JavaScript?
