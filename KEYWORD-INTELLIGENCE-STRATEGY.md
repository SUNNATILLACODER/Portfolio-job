## 2. On-Page Keyword Placement Protocol

To prevent keyword cannibalization and unnatural density:
1. **Never Stuff Keywords in Meta Tags:** Search engines penalize repetitive keyword lists. Focus on natural, context-rich sentences in the editorial copy.
2. **Anchor Link Landmarks:** Ensure internal jump links (`#projects`, `#skills`, `#about`, `#contact`) correspond to logical thematic clusters.
3. **Tri-Lingual Balancing:** The website serves `uz` by default with complete `ru` and `en` parallel content embedded in structured data and translations.
