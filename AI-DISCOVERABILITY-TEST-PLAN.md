## 4. Reality & Limitations Statement

1. **No Absolute Guarantees:** No webmaster, agency, or AI tool can force a proprietary LLM (such as OpenAI or Google) to synthesize a particular answer on demand. AI systems evaluate probabilistic relevance, index freshness, and cross-web authority.
2. **Indexing Lag:** New domains and recent schema changes typically require 1 to 4 weeks of crawler discovery, index validation, and embedding generation before appearing consistently in AI Overviews and SearchGPT citations.
3. **Consistency is the Highest Ranking Factor:** Keeping the same name, location, and project facts across the website, GitHub, and social channels is the most effective driver of accurate entity recognition.
